> Home is the final benchmark for robotics.

I'm Yiyang Liu (刘逸阳), an undergraduate at Xingjian College, Tsinghua University, majoring in Theoretical and Applied Mechanics & Vehicle Engineering. I will continue as a Ph.D. student at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Ya-Qin Zhang.

I lead pre-training at Discover Robotics, where we build foundation models for robots. Some things that I've come to believe: start simple; evaluation matters most (measure how a model grows instead of whether it passes!);  value data by what it teaches the model (not by how clean it looks).

I'm always happy to chat. So please [email](mailto:maxliuyy@gmail.com) me!

## Research Interests
I believe general-purpose robots will emerge from scale, as language models did. I'm interested in the long-term questions behind this bet:

- **Scaling Embodied Intelligence**: What does it take for robot models to scale the way language models did, and to keep generalizing in the open world?
- **From Language to Action**: How can knowledge learned from language, vision and other modalities become physical capability?
- **Robot Data**: Robots have no internet of their own. Where will data at that scale come from, how should it be cleaned, and which data truly matters?
- **Adaptation**: How can each robot keep adapting to the home it lives in, and how should a large model in the cloud and a small one on the robot share that work?
